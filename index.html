New to kendo? Choose Training in the menu: fourteen short lessons with a partner, from footwork and distance to every cut and guard. On touch screens the pad at the bottom moves, the Men button works as F and the Do button as G.

In single-player mode both key sets control Shiro, and Space also strikes. Enter starts, P or Esc pauses, Q in pause returns to the menu, M mutes the sound.

Forward means toward your opponent, back means away. Only the top third of the shinai, the monouchi, scores: from too close the cut does not count. Strike right after a guard for kaeshi-waza, or into the start of the opponent's attack for debana-waza.

Rules

  • Sanbon shobu: the first to take 2 ippon wins. A cut is ippon when at least 2 of the 3 referees raise their flags.
  • A bout lasts 45 seconds. A tie goes to Encho: the first ippon wins. If it is still level, Hantei: the referees decide with their flags.
  • Stepping out of the court is Hansoku. A second hansoku gives the opponent an ippon.
  • Locked close in Tsubazeriai, only hiki-waza works. If nobody acts, the referee calls Wakare and separates you.
  • Every win earns the next grade, from 6th kyu up to the dan grades. After every second win comes Uchikomi-geiko: cut each target the sensei opens.
  • Commands: Hajime begin, Yame stop, Men ari point to men, Nihonme second point, Shobu deciding point, Shobu ari the bout is decided.
